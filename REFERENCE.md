# FinTechCo Brand Guidelines — Quick Reference

## Must-Have Elements
✅ FinTechCo logo on first page/slide
✅ Correct brand colors (exact hex — no approximations)
✅ Inter font family throughout
✅ Consistent formatting and spacing
✅ Professional, clear tone of voice
✅ Accessible contrast ratios (4.5:1 body text minimum)

## Never Use
❌ Competitor names, logos, or comparisons
❌ Unapproved colors, gradients, or neon tones
❌ Decorative, handwriting, or script fonts
❌ Pixelated, stretched, or recolored logos
❌ Jargon, slang, or uncertain language ("maybe", "sort of")
❌ Default Office/Google Docs color schemes on charts

## Accessibility Standards

### Color Contrast
- Body text on white background: Use Ink Black (#1A1A1A) — ratio ~18:1
- Text on FinTechCo Green (#006044) background: Use White (#FFFFFF) — ratio ~7.8:1
- Text on Light Green Tint (#F0F7F4) background: Use Ink Black (#1A1A1A)
- Minimum contrast ratio: **4.5:1** for body text
- Minimum contrast ratio: **3:1** for large text (18pt+ bold, 24pt+ regular)

### Font Sizes
- Minimum body text: 11pt (print), 16px (digital)
- Minimum caption text: 9pt (print), 12px (digital)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Wrong Green**: Using generic green instead of FinTechCo Green #006044 or Bright Green #00A651
2. **Default Office Colors**: Always override chart colors with brand palette
3. **Stretched Logo**: Always maintain aspect ratio
4. **Wrong Font**: Inter only — do not substitute Arial, Calibri, or Times New Roman
5. **Missing Logo**: Always include on first page/slide
6. **Wrong Date Format**: Use "Month DD, YYYY" (October 29, 2026)
7. **Negative Numbers**: Use parentheses ($1,234.56) not minus sign (-$1,234.56)
8. **Vague Language**: Avoid "we think", "maybe", "approximately" — be precise

## Department-Specific Guidelines

### Finance & Investments
- Always right-align numbers in tables
- Use parentheses for negative values: ($1,234.56)
- Color-code gains in Success Green, losses in Error Red
- Include data source citations and as-of dates

### Marketing & Sales
- Can use full secondary color palette
- May include approved imagery from brand asset library
- All CTAs must use FinTechCo Bright Green (#00A651)

### Legal & Compliance
- Use numbered sections (1.0, 1.1, 1.2)
- Include document control block (version, date, owner, classification)
- Apply "Confidential" watermark on sensitive documents
- Error Red for warnings, NOT for decorative use
